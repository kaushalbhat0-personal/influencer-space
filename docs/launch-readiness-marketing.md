## Bottom line

The marketing site sells a credible but **older** version of the platform. The
single most valuable fact — creators keep 100% of every sale — is missing, and
the trust layer is empty or fabricated. Fixing those two things, plus the
contradictory `/faq`, matters more than any copy polish.
