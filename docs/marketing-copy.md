# Marketing Copy — RCCF-IMPLEMENTATION-73

## The copy philosophy

> Market the outcome, not the mechanism. The AI is the engine, not the headline.

| Before | After |
| --- | --- |
| "Our AI builds your entire business" (hero subhead) | "Paste your YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok profile and launch a storefront you fully own" |
| Primary CTA "Start" | **"Build My Storefront — Free"** |
| "2-minute setup" trust | **"15-day free trial"** |
| Title "AI-Powered Creator Business Platform" | **"Turn your content into a business"** |

## Hero value bullets (real, creator-facing)

- **Keep 100% of every sale**
- **Launch in minutes**
- **Sell products, services, courses & bookings**
- **Built for Indian creators**

## Smart-platform messaging (no internal names)

| Internal runtime | Marketing copy |

## What was fixed

- `/faq` "What are the transaction fees?" → **"What does it cost to sell?" —
  "Creators keep 100% of every sale. CreatorStore never takes a transaction
  fee."**
- `/faq` "Is there a free plan?" → **"Is there a free trial?" — 15 days, no
  credit card.**
- About "Join thousands of creators" → "Join creators turning their content into
  a business."
- Root metadata description: outcome-first, no leading AI framing.
- FAQPage JSON-LD Q/As written in the same creator-first voice.
